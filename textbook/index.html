Data Structures & Algorithms

An online textbook, one self-contained chapter per topic.

Every chapter states its problem, develops the method, works a full example step by step with each case of the algorithm exercised, argues the cost rather than asserting it, and ends in exercises with solutions. Chapters are independent: each names and links what it depends on, so they can be read in any order and assembled into a syllabus freely.

Data structure
How data is stored, and what each operation costs.
Algorithm
A procedure that solves one problem, with its design paradigm noted where it has one.
Design paradigm
A method for building algorithms, and the conditions under which it works.
Foundation
Analysis tools, problem statements, and the theorems that license the algorithms.
