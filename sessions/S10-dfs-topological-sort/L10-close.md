## Recap

- DFS stamps **pre** and **post** times; the intervals nest or are disjoint
- edge types by the color of the far end: **tree**, **back**, **forward**, **cross**
- a digraph has a **cycle** if and only if DFS finds a **back edge**
- **topological sort**: Kahn's in-degree queue, or DFS reverse post-order
- **SCCs**: DFS on G for reverse post-order, then DFS on the transpose in that order
